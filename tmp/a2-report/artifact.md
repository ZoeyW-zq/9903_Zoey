## Page system

- A4 portrait, 8.27 x 11.69 inches.
- Margins: 1 inch on all sides.
- One column, no first-page or odd/even header variants.
- No decorative page furniture in the source.

## Typography

- Main family: Aptos.
- Report title: 24 pt, bold, black.
- Experience title: 20 pt, regular, black.
- Section headings: 24 pt, bold, black.
- Body: 12 pt, black, left aligned, approximately 1.15 line spacing.
- Source headings are directly formatted rather than true heading styles. The final draft may use real Heading styles while preserving the same visible hierarchy.

## Slot map

- All instructional body text is replaceable and must be removed.
- All report content slots are editable.
- Unknown student metadata, WebGL URL, collaboration evidence, headset-test evidence, and unverified asset URLs must remain visibly marked placeholders.
- The Narrative State Network may be inserted as a generated diagram because the template explicitly requests a legible NSN.
- The template's sample collaboration screenshot and sample asset list are instructional examples and must not be represented as the student's evidence.

## Fidelity gates

- Preserve A4 geometry, one-inch margins, Aptos family, black hierarchy, and the source section order.
- Keep the report within the 500-3500 word requirement; references and placeholder notes should be clearly separated from the main argument.
- Do not invent links, collaboration events, testing results, or asset provenance.
- Final DOCX must render without clipping, overlapping text, broken tables, missing glyphs, or isolated headings.
